The 20-second test Load this page, switch off Wi-Fi and mobile data, then load your file. It still works, because there's nowhere for your data to go.
Does my data leave my phone or laptop?

No. The page reads your file inside your browser. There is no server that could receive it, no account and no analytics. Close the tab and it's gone.

Will Safaricom, my bank or my employer know?

No. Pesadithi never contacts anyone. It reads the SMS and statements you already have. It isn't made by or linked to any wallet or bank.

Why should I trust a stranger's website?

You shouldn't have to. Every line is open source on GitHub, the page blocks itself from sending data anywhere, and you can run the offline test above. It's made by Mark Kaigwa, in public.

Is the PDF password my M-PESA PIN?

Never. Statements are locked with a one-off code sent by SMS. Nothing here will ever ask for your PIN. If anything does, close it.

What if someone picks up my phone afterwards?

Nothing stays unless you tick "Remember". Even then only your category labels and targets are kept, with people stored as scrambled codes. "Clear my data" wipes it in two taps.

I'm a bit embarrassed by my numbers.

Most people are. Nobody sees them but you, and the story is about timing, not judgement: when money arrives, how fast it goes, what borrowing costs. It never scolds.

What if it reads something wrong?

You'll see a list of every SMS it couldn't read, and estimates are labelled as estimates. Your official statement is always the final word.

Is it free? What's the catch?

It's free, with no ads and nothing to sell you. It's a lab: an experiment in showing people their own money clearly.

Before you start, what this can and can't do
  • It works best with M-PESA (Kenya). MTN MoMo and Airtel Money are in beta: messages vary by country, so some may not be read.

Shared money In design

Your money is rarely just yours. Pesadithi is learning to tell that story too.

Most of us carry other people in our wallets: a partner, a flat, our parents, a chama. Each role asks a different question, and each needs a different line on what gets shared.

How sharing will work, whoever it's with

  1. Each person runs it on their own phone. Nobody uploads a statement to anyone else.
  2. You choose an envelope. Rent, school fees, Mum's upkeep, the chama. Only lines you put in it count.
  3. You send a card, not a statement. Totals for that envelope only, as an image or a short code. Nothing else crosses.
  4. Either side can stop. Delete the card and the shared view is gone.

Which role should come first? Tell Mark on X or open an issue on GitHub.

    Privacy and risks
    
    
    What happens to your data, in plain words
    
    
    This page: your file is opened and read by JavaScript inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no server that could receive it. The page loads only its own files: fonts, a chart library, a PDF reader and, if you attach screenshots, a text reader, all served from the same place. You can check this: load the page, switch off Wi-Fi or mobile data, then load your file. It still works. For a PDF, choose the PDF route once while online so the PDF reader loads first.
    
    
    Nothing is saved unless you tick "Remember my sorting and targets". Even then, only your category choices and budget targets are kept in this browser, with each person stored as a scrambled code rather than a name. Your transactions are never kept. "Clear my data" at the bottom of the page removes everything.
